X Reel Maker

Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

X Reel Maker is a personal desktop app that runs on its owner's own computer. Only its owner uses it.

Google data the app uses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app asks for two permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload), to upload the videos the owner chooses to the owner's channel.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly), only to show the name of the connected channel.

The app doesn't read, change or delete any other YouTube or Google data.

Where data is kept

The sign-in key Google returns is stored only in a file on the owner's computer. The app has no server. It sends data only to Google (for YouTube) and Meta (for the owner's own Instagram account), and only when the owner posts a video.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with anyone else, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. X Reel Maker's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps & services. Deleting the app's files from the computer deletes everything it stored.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
